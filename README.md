# 🏦 BranchPulse AI

### Intelligent Branch Service Load and Customer Experience Optimizer

BranchPulse AI is a predictive and prescriptive banking branch optimization platform developed for the **TCS AI Hackathon**.

It forecasts branch waiting times **30 and 60 minutes ahead**, measures branch load severity, diagnoses operational bottlenecks, prioritizes feasible actions for branch managers, and helps customers avoid unnecessary branch visits through digital-service and lower-load branch guidance.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Bank branches often experience:

- Uneven customer traffic
- Long waiting times
- Staff overload
- Poor counter utilization
- Complex service bottlenecks
- Customers visiting branches for digitally available services
- Inconsistent service experience across branches

Traditional dashboards generally show current queue information but do not answer:

> **What is likely to happen next, why is it happening, and what should the branch manager do about it?**

BranchPulse AI addresses this by moving through:
